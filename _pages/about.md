About
======
I am a Joint Ph.D. student at Nanjing University of Science and Technology (NJUST) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU), advised by Prof. Dacheng Tao and Prof. Chen Gong. My research focuses on machine learning, with interests in Knowledge Distillation, model compression, time series forecasting, and Learning with Label Noise.
